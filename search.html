Pokémon GO Search Strings

Build a string for the game's own search box. Click a chip once to require it, twice to rule it out.

 

This mixes , and &, and the game's search has no brackets to say which binds first. Check it matches what you meant before trusting it on a mass transfer.

⭐ Favourites

Name or species

    Partial names match, so char finds Charmander and Charizard. Two letters bring up the species that match, and ↓ then Enter takes one. Each species is followed by its family: taking Charmander (family) writes +charmander, the game's shorthand for a species and the rest of its evolutionary line. A name you have typed yourself becomes one of these on a comma or Enter — a nickname works as well as a species. Chosen names come out whole rather than a letter at a time: ⌫ in the empty part of the box takes the last one off, and ✕ takes any of them.

    Ranges

    A box left empty falls back to that range's own limit, so one end is enough.

    The syntax is Pokémon GO's own, as typed into the search box on the Pokémon storage screen. Everything runs in your browser, and the link updates as you build so a string can be shared or bookmarked.
